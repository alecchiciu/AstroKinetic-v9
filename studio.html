04 / INTERACTIVE FLIGHT & BIOMECHANICAL PARAMETERS

Calibrate Variables & Test Biomechanical Responses

Every parameter can be adjusted in real time. Hover or read the explanations below to understand how each variable informs astronaut health monitoring.

CALIBRATION ENGINE
🚀 Propulsion Impulse (N·s) 12.4 N·s

Integral of normal force over push-off duration (∫ F dt). Determines takeoff velocity across the cabin. Higher values indicate energetic locomotion.

〰️ 8–12 Hz Micro-Tremor (mm/s²) 0.82 mm/s²

Involuntary physiological neuromuscular oscillations in the 8–12 Hz band. Primary biomarker for central nervous system fatigue and sensorimotor ataxia.

⚡ Autonomic EDA / GSR (μS) 3.2 μS

Palmar skin conductance driven by sympathetic nervous system activation. Elevates instantly under acute cognitive demand, anxiety, or stress.

⏱️ Pre-Propulsion Dwell (ms) 290 ms

Contact dwell latency before takeoff initiation (Hesitation Index). Reflects motor planning latency, cognitive uncertainty, or tactile confidence.

📏 Handrail Dimensions (mm) 600 × 32 mm

Physical length and cylindrical diameter. Modifying length adjusts total sensor count (150 cells for 600 mm, 120 cells for 480 mm).

🔌 Hardware USB Connection Synthetic Stream
